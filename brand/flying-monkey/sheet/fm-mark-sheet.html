A flying monkey, seen head-on, wings spread. One silhouette with knockouts. The construction the Bacardi bat has used since 1862: a symmetrical creature with its wings out, and one asymmetry that makes it a character rather than a symbol. Here that is the tail, curling under to the left. The face is a skull-white heart with hollow eyes and a row of teeth, which is where the macabre lives; everything else is drawn as a living animal.
